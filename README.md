## Projects

**CAELIX: frictionless-checkout smart cart** · 2025 to 2026 · team of 3  
A shopping cart that checks every item three ways (the scanned barcode, the weight change on its load cells, and a camera running object detection) and flags any disagreement to a store dashboard.  
`ESP32` `HX711` `YOLOv8` `Flask` `Firebase` · [Repository](https://github.com/Khanakag-17/CAELIX_Smart_Cart_System) · [Live site](https://livecart-1407.web.app/) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u3Lkdf7qy1o)

**Crop Cure: plant disease classification** · 2024 · team of 4  
A convolutional network trained from scratch on PlantVillage that tells apart 38 classes of healthy and diseased leaves (94.74% validation accuracy) and runs in the browser through TensorFlow.js.  
`TensorFlow` `Keras` `TensorFlow.js` · [Live app](https://crop-cure-livid.vercel.app/)

**Multi-class animal detection** · 2024 · team of 4  
A YOLOv8 model fine-tuned to find 20 animal species, trained on a 2,000-image dataset our team collected and labelled by hand in Roboflow.  
`YOLOv8` `Roboflow` `Python` · [Dataset on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/notaashman/multiclass-animal-detection)
